# Supply Chain Analysis and Compliance Submission

## Submission Overview

This submission presents an end-to-end review of supply chain issues, covering business problem identification, stakeholder communication, governance planning, quantitative risk assessment, and executive compliance reporting.

The files are arranged to show a clear progression from **issue identification** through **analysis and remediation planning** to **executive decision support**.

> **Important publication note:** The analysis is conditionally ready for internal review. External publication should remain on hold until the BIND567 timeline discrepancy and the definition of a delayed component are resolved and approved by the appropriate data owner.

### Task 3: Data Analysis and Supply Chain Risk Assessment

**Objective**  
Use the supplied data to evaluate delivery performance, component exposure, supplier performance, data integrity, and appropriate mitigation actions.

**Deliverables**

1. **Supply chain analytical workbook**
   - Provides a performance summary based on 50 source records.
   - Compares average delivery timelines by risk level.
   - Reviews CARB123, BIND567, and COAT890 component performance.
   - Compares supplier delivery, reliability, defect, inspection, and lead-time metrics.
   - Retains the detailed dataset and component analysis for traceability.

2. **Supply chain risk assessment report**
   - Summarises material findings for management review.
   - Assesses component-specific and supplier-level exposure.
   - Explains compliance and data-integrity implications.
   - Separates supported observations from hypotheses requiring further evidence.
   - Recommends general and targeted mitigation strategies.
   - Documents assurance checks and publication-readiness conditions.

**Files**
- `Task -3 Supply Chain report.xlsx`
- `Task 3 - Supply chain risk assessment data Supply Chain Issue Analysis Report.docx`

**Key findings**
- Medium-risk records have the longest average delivery timeline, so the risk-scoring logic requires validation rather than an assumption that the labels are incorrect.
- COAT890 is the highest immediate component concern based on its 84.2% average on-time rate across five records.
- BIND567 has a 64-day timeline in the Dataset worksheet and a 10-day timeline in the Analysis worksheet; this discrepancy must be resolved before publication.
- The dataset has no dedicated delay-status field, so the current delayed-component analysis includes all records for CARB123, BIND567, and COAT890.

## Data Assurance and Known Limitations

Before relying on or externally publishing the analysis, reviewers should address the following:

- **BIND567 timeline conflict:** The Dataset worksheet reports 64 days, while the Analysis worksheet reports 10 days.
- **Delay definition:** The source dataset does not contain a dedicated delay-status field.
- **Risk classification:** Delivery duration alone does not explain the assigned risk levels; the full scoring logic should be confirmed.
- **Internal performance target:** The supplied source does not evidence an approved 95% on-time delivery target.
- **BIND567 sample size:** Its component result is based on one record and should not be treated as representative until additional evidence is available.
- **Publication status:** The analysis is conditionally ready for internal review. External publication should remain on hold until the BIND567 discrepancy and the delayed-component definition are resolved and approved by the data owner.
